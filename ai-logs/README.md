The [manifest](manifest.json) records the capture time, original source paths, byte
counts and SHA-256 hashes. The JSONL files are copied byte-for-byte, without filtering,
redaction or rewriting. This is a snapshot of the logs at the recorded capture time;
later messages in the live conversation require refreshing the export.

Download the raw files to inspect their JSONL records. The existing
[export script](../scripts/export_ai_logs.py) refreshes the copies from the original
local sessions listed in the manifest.
